Mean Reversion Strategy for Crypto

Market: Bitcoin/Ethereum daily timeframe

Entry:
- Price drops 4% from 24-hour high (mean reversion signal)
- RSI(14) falls below 35 (oversold)
- Trading volume is 150% of 20-day average
- Price is above 50-day exponential moving average (trend filter)

Exit:
- Take Profit: +2.5% from entry
- Stop Loss: -1.5% from entry (tight stops for mean reversion)
- Time Stop: Close after 5 days if not filled

Position Sizing:
- Risk 0.75% of account per trade (conservative for crypto)
- Position size = (Account_Risk / Stop_Loss_Distance)
- Maximum position size capped at 5% of account

Risk Management:
- Maximum 2 concurrent positions (reduce correlation risk)
- Daily loss limit: -3% of daily starting capital
- Account drawdown limit: -8% from peak
- No trading during major news events

Confirmation Rules:
- Filter out trades in low volatility periods (ATR too small)
- Require price to be in bottom 30% of 20-day range
- Skip if major technical levels are being tested

Notes:
This is a mean reversion strategy that exploits oversold conditions
while maintaining trend confirmation. Use 3+ years of backtest data.
